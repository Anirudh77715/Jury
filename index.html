Why it matters

India's EV boom runs on batteries that are hard to grade.

2.45 MEVs sold in India in FY26Source: Autocar Professional (Vahan data)
> 91%of EV sales are 2- and 3-wheelersSource: IESA, FY26
35–50%battery share of an e-scooter's costSource: MeraEV 2026 (indicative)
5–14%higher cost of e-2W/3W loans vs petrolSource: RMI, 2024
100%import dependence for lithium, cobalt, nickelSource: Ministry of Mines data
~80%end-of-life battery scrap via informal channelsSource: industry estimate, 2026
128 GWhLi-ion batteries projected for recycling by 2030Source: NITI Aayog, 2022
₹1,500 Crcritical-mineral recycling incentiveSource: Union Cabinet, Sep 2025

India's draft Battery Pack Aadhaar (MoRTH, Dec 2025) includes a State of Health field. GreenScore is designed to fill it independently.

Live health check

One charging session in. One honest estimate out.

Pick a simulated pack or upload your own log. The demo estimator turns voltage into state of charge, compares it with the charge that flowed in, and refuses when the signal is not good enough.

  1. 1

    Record one ordinary charge

    Pack voltage, current and temperature, once a second, while the pack charges normally.

  2. 2

    Estimate health with a band

    Usable capacity from the slope of state of charge against charge added, with a 90% band.

  3. 3

    Refuse, or issue a passport

    Nine quality rules decide. Scored or refused, the result becomes a hashed, shareable record.

    Who benefits

    One estimate, four decisions.

    Click or press Enter on a card to flip it.

    Measure → Trust → Circulate

    From first owner to formal recycling.

    1. Battery in use: health measured, not guessed.
    2. Second life: stationary storage cabinet.
    3. Formal recycling: recover lithium, cobalt, nickel.
    4. Manufacturing: minerals back into new batteries.

    Verified health decides the next life of every battery.
